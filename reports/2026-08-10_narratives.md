## WDC — Western Digital
**Bull case:**
- AI-driven demand surge for HDDs: bulls (incl. a Cloudflare-sourced take on AI-agent data growth) call it "super bullish on HDDs" for WDC/STX.
- Q4 FY2026 beat: revenue $3.75B (+44% YoY), adj. EPS $3.56 (+109%), gross margin 54.4%; Q1 FY2027 guide also above consensus. Nearline HDD exabytes shipped up ~22-23% YoY. BofA raised PT to $415 (Buy).
- Structural tailwinds: long-term supply agreements being discussed through 2029-2031; 40TB ePMR shipping now, 44TB HAMR ramping H1 2027 (JPMorgan called this a structural nearline demand validator).

**Bear case:**
- "Beat but expectations failure": stock still fell ~16-17% over two days because exabyte growth (+22%) missed the >25% pace investors had priced in after the stock tripled this year; pricing power and margin durability now questioned.
- Valuation/cyclical risk: even post-selloff, ~21.6x FY2027 / ~42x FY2026 adj. EPS bakes in ~49% revenue growth and near-doubled EPS — aggressive for cyclical hardware, and 89% of revenue is cloud/hyperscaler-concentrated.

**Debate in one line:** Whether the AI-HDD demand story is still accelerating fast enough to justify a stock that already tripled, or whether this quarter's "good but not great" exabyte growth marks a deceleration.
**Chatter volume:** Elevated — dominated by post-earnings reaction (earnings were Aug 6-7).

## MU — Micron Technology
**Bull case:**
- Structural AI/HBM demand: Micron holds ~$22B in customer deals with constraints seen persisting 2+ years, decoupling it from the old memory cycle.
- Explosive growth trajectory: sequential revenue jumps cited (FQ2 $23.86B → FQ3 $41.46B, per one thread) plus fab/packaging ramps (Idaho, Taiwan, Singapore) and 1γ EUV DRAM + HBM4 ramping 2026-2027, backed by take-or-pay agreements.
- Catalyst watch: Micron presents at the KeyBanc Technology Leadership Forum (Aug 11), flagged as a possible intra-quarter guide-raise moment (a similar 2025 event moved the stock 4-6%).

**Bear case:**
- Citi cut its price target from $1,400 to $1,150 (kept Buy), citing an expected DRAM/NAND pricing slowdown peaking in 2027, and framed Micron as more commodity-exposed than SK Hynix's HBM-heavy mix.
- A "rotation" argument: some traders see photonics names (e.g. LITE) as offering more/longer growth runway from a smaller base than memory, pulling attention/capital away from MU.

**Debate in one line:** Is Micron's HBM/AI demand structurally locked-in for years, or is it still a cyclical memory name facing a 2027 pricing slowdown?
**Chatter volume:** Low — sparse, scattered mentions rather than dedicated MU threads; highest-engagement relevant post had only ~121 likes.

**Debate in one line:** Whether Palantir's commercial-AI acceleration justifies a valuation that already prices in years of flawless execution, or whether it's now priced to perfection with no margin of safety.
