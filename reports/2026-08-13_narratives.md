### 1. Does the AI compute supercycle keep compounding — or is it a bubble propped up by financial engineering?
**Names in play:** NVDA, AVGO, CRWV, NBIS, IREN, ORCL (core); PLTR, CBRS, TSM (secondary)

**Bull side:**
- NVDA's $500B+ financing consortium with Wall Street (Apollo, Blackstone) and residual-value guarantees (up to 25%/$125B) reframes GPUs as a financable asset class, like aircraft leasing — old chips (A100s 6yrs old, V100s 7.5yrs) still earn via inference/resale, with A100 rental rates +25% this year — @MilkRoadStocks (264k views).
- NBIS: Q2 deals averaging >$20M ACV/MW (landmark deals $20-25M), TCV signed +4x QoQ, ~70% of Q2 deals carrying prepayments covering 50-60% of capex — @InvestNorthwise (196 bookmarks). IREN delivered its first 50MW Horizon 1 site to Microsoft under a $9.7B contract with NVIDIA "Exemplar Cloud" validation.
- AVGO parallel financing (~20GW/$35B+ tranche, $30B guarantees) with AI semi revenue +143% YoY; bulls call these deals a demand signal, not a weakness.

**Bear side:**
- Michael Burry's core thesis — aggressive GPU depreciation schedules understate the true cost of the buildout — keeps resurfacing; he's now shorting NBIS and ORCL even as they rally, calling it "the top" — @burrytracker (725k views on one update).
- The need for outside financing/residual guarantees is itself read as a tell that the "easy money phase" is ending, with longer-term oversupply risk once backstops are needed to move product — flagged even by bullish desks (Wolfe Research).
- CBRS's Q2 miss ($180M rev vs. ~$194M est., stock -17% premarket) despite a $25.4B backlog shows how quickly the tape punishes any wobble in the AI-hardware growth story.

**The crux:** whether multi-year-old GPUs still earning full price, and financing structures that expand the buyer pool, are proof of durable compute demand — or a sign the economics only work with escalating leverage and guarantees that come due later.

**Chatter:** Elevated — the single most cross-referenced narrative of the sweep, spanning chips, neocloud, and mega-cap sections; multiple 100k-700k+ view threads.

**Bear side:**

- Possible AI-model-ROI commoditization (cheaper/open-weight models needing less compute) is flagged as a longer-tail risk to end demand for the whole group.

**The crux:** whether locked-in, multi-year customer capex commitments outrun the risks (China competition, an earlier-than-expected supply response, AI-ROI doubt), or those risks cap the visibility bulls are pricing in.

**Chatter:** Moderate-to-high, concentrated around AMAT's earnings print and LRCX's JPM-forum commentary; ONTO/CAMT lower-volume but strongly one-sided bullish on their own prints.

**Bull side:**
- Explosive backlog numbers across the group: GEV's $176B total backlog / 116GW gas-turbine book (sold out through 2030, already locking 2031 slots); ETN's electrical backlog +103% YoY with data-center orders +85% YoY; LGN backlog +105% YoY to $5.67B.

- The framing: compute itself is a "melting ice cube" (fast-depreciating), while power/grid/turbine assets behave more like real estate — a safer way to play the same AI buildout.

**Bear side:**
- Physical buildout is slowed by permitting/red tape and grid-connection lag even as demand looks "insatiable" — a concise bear framing repeated across the sector: "Bull: insatiable demand for compute. Bear: building DCs is slow and slowed further by red tape/energy lag."
- GEV's own bull case contains a ceiling: most 2027-2030 production slots (and over half of 2031) are expected sold out by year-end, and customers rarely commit beyond ~5 years — so incremental new-order growth has a near-term cap even without a demand collapse.
- Valuation/technical caution across the group (STRL head-and-shoulders pattern hitting its measured-move target; a $12.7M discretionary insider sale at FIX flagged as a bearish signal; Bloom's scandium-supply short report still not fully verified).

**The crux:** if GPUs really do last through 2029 (debate #1), does that reduce the case for physical/power infra as the "safer" AI-durability trade, or do both keep winning together as the buildout simply gets bigger?

**Chatter:** Moderate-to-elevated — BE/FCEL the loudest single catalyst of the window (dozens of posts, up to 175k views); ETN/VRT/GEV/LGN/ENS driven by earnings and backlog data rather than a single viral thread.
