### 1. Does old AI compute actually last — and does that make NVDA/neocloud safer or scarier?
**Names in play:** NVDA, CRWV, NBIS, IREN, ORCL (core); AVGO, MRVL, TSM (secondary)

**Bull side:**
- CoreWeave's Q2 print ($2.6B revenue, ~100%+ YoY, narrower loss) showed A100 GPUs from 2020 still under contract through **2029**, read as a direct rebuttal of Michael Burry's depreciation/short thesis — @StockSavvyShay (~950 likes/83k+ views).
- Bulls tie this to durable infra ROI: managed inference ~$100M ARR, non-GPU lines >$400M run-rate, backlog $104B→$129.2B QoQ, power capacity 3.7GW→4.2GW, plus a $500B private-credit financing deal that keeps buyer capacity off NVDA's own balance sheet.
- Read-through: eases funding/circularity fears and lifted NBIS (+5% AH) and IREN on the neocloud-as-NVDA's-AI-factory framing.

**Bear side:**
- The underlying Burry short thesis (aggressive 3–5yr GPU write-downs killing legacy revenue) is being cited far more than actively defended right now — bears were largely on the back foot this window.
- Where skepticism showed up: still-massive neocloud losses/rising capex, ORCL trading red despite sector momentum (read as a "hyperscalers don't need third-party capacity" tell), and broader worry that financing structures (private credit, vendor pre-pays) are circular lending dressed up as demand.

**The crux:** whether multi-year-old GPUs still earning full price through 2029 is proof of a durable compute supercycle, or just deferred recognition of a bill (depreciation, financing risk) that comes due later.

**Chatter:** Elevated — the single highest-engagement thread of the sweep (80k+ views on the anchor post); dozens of follow-on posts across chips and neocloud sections.

**The crux:** are the drawdowns forced technical/flow selling on top of an intact, contract-locked supercycle, or the market front-running a real 2027+ supply response (CXMT, general normalization)?

**Chatter:** Low-to-moderate — dozens of posts, mostly under 200 views with a few 2k–15k spikes; concentrated among ~10-15 recurring finance/tech accounts rather than broad retail.
