**The crux:** whether Nvidia's Aug 26 earnings (and follow-through hyperscaler capex commentary into year-end) confirm demand is still outrunning supply, or mark the point where AI capex growth visibly decelerates.

**Chatter:** elevated.

**Chatter:** elevated.

**The crux:** whether contracted backlogs are durable, arm's-length revenue or partly self-referential financing between Nvidia and its own customers — and whether regulators (Texas) constrain the build-out regardless.
**Chatter:** elevated.
